# Screening criteria for non-NYU PhD programs

A program reaches the longlist only if it clears **all five** hard screens. Soft preferences are scored after, never instead.

---

## Hard screens (pass/fail)

### H1 — Fully funded
Guaranteed multi-year stipend + tuition remission for admitted PhD students, including international students on the standard package.
**Fail if:** self-funded, funding is competitive after admission, or the only reliable funding is an NIH T32 / training grant restricted to US citizens or permanent residents (common trap in biomedical informatics).

### H2 — Degree + healthcare thesis route
Degree in Data Science, CS, ML, Statistics, Biomedical Informatics, Biostatistics, or equivalent, **and** active faculty publishing in clinical ML / EHR / health-AI, ideally with an affiliated medical centre and a plausible EHR-data path.
**Fail if:** pure theory department with no health collaborator, or a health program with no serious ML faculty.

### H3 — Admissible with this record
MSc Data Science (Uppsala) + BE Software Engineering (NUST, Gold Medal) is enough. GRE 158V/164Q/4.0W is available.
**Fail if:** requires a specific US-style undergraduate major he does not have, or has a hard GRE floor that 164Q will not clear for a competitive international CS applicant (flag rather than auto-fail if borderline; many health-informatics programs are more forgiving than top CS).

### H4 — Liveable without a car
Walk + transit and/or safe cycling are enough for daily life. Rate High / Medium / Low; **Low fails.**
Honest examples: NYC, Boston, SF/Berkeley, Chicago, Seattle, DC, Philadelphia → usually High. Many US university towns with a bus and a bike → Medium (acceptable). Car-compulsory sprawl → Fail.

### H5 — International-student friendly in practice
The program actually admits and funds international PhD students in this research area. Check recent cohort composition or FAQ language, not just a non-discrimination statement.

## Soft preferences (score 1–5 each after hard screens pass)

| Code | Preference | Why |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Faculty overlap with provenance / multi-site generalisation / EHR foundation models / causal-invariance / clinical interpretability | Direct thesis fit |
| S2 | Affiliated hospital system with research EHR access | Makes the project real |
| S3 | Stipend-to-rent ratio survivable | Matters for five years |
| S4 | Explicit health-AI or biomedical-informatics track | Signal that the department wants this work |
| S5 | Culture compatible with his voice (deployment-minded, not prestige-performing) | Application will read better |

---

## Fit score (1–10)

Holistic, after hard screens. Anchor:
- 9–10: multiple faculty who could advise this exact thesis; medical centre; High car-free; strong international funding.
- 7–8: at least one clear advisor fit; workable city; funded.
- 5–6: possible but would need a rewritten thesis angle.
- Below 5: do not shortlist.
