Applicant frame: Pakistani national (F-1); MSc Data Science, Uppsala; BE Software Engineering, NUST (Gold Medal); GRE 158V / 164Q; IELTS 8.5. Needs **car-free-liveable** city. Hard screens from [`01-screening-criteria.md`](01-screening-criteria.md).

## Methodology note

- **Screens applied (all required):** H1 fully funded package for internationals on the *standard* path (flag NIH T32 / NLM T15 traps); H2 DS/CS/ML/Stats/BMI (or equivalent) with a real healthcare thesis route + affiliated medical centre; H3 admissible with this record; H4 car-free liveability High or Medium only; H5 international-friendly in practice.
- **Sources:** Official program / graduate-school pages fetched or searched in Aug 2026. Facts are tagged **SOURCED** (URL) or **UNCERTAIN**. Stipend *amounts* and Fall 2027 deadlines that are not yet posted are written **not found** rather than invented.
- **Deadlines:** Prefer Fall 2027 when published. Otherwise label **last-cycle** (Fall 2026 entry) and expect similar timing; re-check when portals open.
- **Coverage vs accuracy:** Prefer fewer verified rows over padded inventiveness. Strongly considered list (MIT, Harvard, Stanford BDS, CMU, JHU, UW Allen/BIME, Columbia, Cornell Tech, Berkeley–UCSF CPH, UCSF, Michigan, UPenn, Duke, Northwestern, Chicago, Vanderbilt, BU, Mount Sinai, Yale, Emory) is accounted for in either the table or **Rejected**.
- **Car-free ratings:** Honest. Dense transit metros → High. Campus + bus/bike towns that work for daily life without a car → Medium. Sprawl metros where daily life without a car fails → Low (auto-fail).
