# NYU CDS PhD — Medical School Track — program mechanics

Fall 2027 cycle. Compiled from GSAS Statement of Academic Purpose instructions, `cds.nyu.edu` pages, and the pasted portal walkthrough (`Cover Letters/Inidivual Program Application Site Instructions/NYU- Data Science.txt`, matches this file verbatim in substance). Full source trail in `history/requirements.md` and `history/nyu-program-and-lab-intel.md` §3.

## Portal logistics

- **Application site:** https://apply.gsas.nyu.edu/apply/
- **Deadline:** 3 Dec 2026, all materials by 5:00 PM ET.
- **Fee:** $130, waiver applied for (decision by 24 Nov 2026 at the latest) — have a backup payment method ready regardless.
- **GRE:** not required (considered if submitted).
- **GSAS rule:** only one GSAS application at a time (cannot dual-apply MSDS + PhD).
- **Documents:**
  - Statement of Academic Purpose (required) — ≤2 double-spaced pages. No separate word-count override from Data Science / GSAS. Cannot be revised after submission.
  - Personal History Statement (optional, recommended for this applicant's thesis) — ≤2 double-spaced pages, must not duplicate the SoP.
  - 3 letters of recommendation.
- **Upload mechanics:** "Choose File" → "Continue" → preview/delete only before final submission. No edits after full submission.

## Track structure

- Medical School Track students complete the standard CDS PhD curriculum (72 credits, GPA ≥3.0; six semesters of research rotations) **plus** SoM electives and SoM faculty collaboration.
- Start with ≥1 CDS mentor (PhD Advisory Group) **and** ≥1 Medical School mentor — dual-advisor design confirmed by Sumit Chopra in the Fall 2026 admissions info session. Not a single-lab direct-admit; rotations still apply.
- Comprehensive exam: 4-page first-year report + 10-min talk by end of 2nd semester (Fall 2024+ rule).
- Depth Qualifying Exam (DQE) by May 15 of 4th semester, ≤2 attempts.
- Teaching requirement: 2 CDS courses by end of year 4.
- Dissertation proposal guideline: by end of year 3.

## Funding

- Tuition + competitive stipend for up to 5 years. 2025–2026 nine-month stipend was $41,832; exact Fall 2027 figure not yet published (reviewed yearly).
