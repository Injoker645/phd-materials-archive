## Bioethicist

**On the hook:** The softened version ("the difference is that one of those agents is actually auditable") is better than "who is allowed to be unaccountable." It makes a resourcing claim rather than a moral accusation. Still watch the implied equivalence: saying doctor and model have the same contamination is a strong claim about clinicians. Soften further if a reader flinches — "related contamination" / "overlapping sources of habit."

**On the draft:** The bioethics contact is used cleanly. Do not let it inflate into "I have a background in bioethics."

**Verdict:** Pass with a one-word watch on "same contamination."
