# NYU Statement of Academic Purpose — requirements checklist

> Source: NYU GSAS Statement of Academic Purpose instructions (Data Science PhD). Deadline 3 December 2026 for Fall 2027. Fee $130. GRE optional.

## Portal logistics (confirmed 2026-09-22, portal open)

- **Deadline:** 3 Dec 2026, all application materials submitted online by **5:00 PM Eastern Time**. If 3 Dec falls on a weekend/US federal holiday, deadline rolls to next business day (not applicable this cycle — 3 Dec 2026 is a Thursday).
- **Fee waiver:** applied for; decision by **24 Nov 2026** at the latest. If denied, pay the $130 fee before the 3 Dec cutoff — do not wait until decision day to have a backup payment method ready.
- **Documents on file, no upload yet:**
  - Statement of Academic Purpose (required) — ≤2 double-spaced pages, no word count override specified for this dept. See spine below. Cannot be revised after submission — proof carefully before upload.
  - Personal History Statement (optional, recommended for this thesis) — ≤2 double-spaced pages, must not duplicate the SOP. Draft in [`personal-history.md`](personal-history.md).
- **Upload mechanics:** "Choose File" → "Continue" → can preview/delete only by returning to that page before final submission. No edits after full submission.

## The four asks (every draft must hit all four)

1. **Past and present work as it relates to the intended field of study.** Not a CV dump — a selection of experiences that make the research direction feel inevitable.
2. **Educational objectives.** What he wants to learn / build in the PhD.
3. **Career goals.** Academia-vs-industry can stay honestly undecided; the through-line is applied healthcare AI whose results reach a clinic.
4. **Intellectual and professional reasons for choosing this field, and why the work is best done at NYU GSAS.** Specific faculty, specific models, specific data access — not prestige language.

## Hard constraints

- **Maximum two double-spaced pages ≈ 550–650 words.** Selection is the whole problem.
- Voice: Register A from [`../applicant/voice-samples.md`](../applicant/voice-samples.md). No em dashes. British spelling. No "I am writing to apply." No prestige compliments. Hedge by naming checkable gaps.
- Claims: only `USABLE` items from [`../applicant/claim-inventory.md`](../applicant/claim-inventory.md). No KFSH electrophoresis pilot. No unverified statistics from deep-dive 07 until [`../research/verification/algorithm-aversion-claims.md`](../research/verification/algorithm-aversion-claims.md) exists.
- Research claims: only the safe anchors in [`../research/verification/00-verification-log.md`](../research/verification/00-verification-log.md). Thesis version: **v4**.
