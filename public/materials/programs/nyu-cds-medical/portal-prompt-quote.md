# NYU GSAS — Statement prompts (official)

**Portal:** https://apply.gsas.nyu.edu/apply/  
**Reconciled:** 2026-10-27 (and re-checked the same day against both sources below).

There are two related texts. The **department / academic-purpose prompt** is the one the SoP must answer. The **application-page framing** is the GSAS upload-page blurb that sits around it. Neither states a word count — only **two double-spaced pages**.

---

## 1. Official Statement of Academic Purpose prompt (program / department)

> In a concisely written statement, please describe your past and present work as it relates to your intended field of study, your educational objectives, and your career goals. In addition, please include your intellectual and professional reasons for choosing your field of study and why your studies/research can best be done at the Graduate School of Arts and Science at NYU. The statement should not exceed two double-spaced pages.

## 2. Application-page framing (GSAS upload page)

> **Statement of Purpose**  
> We ask that you please tell us about yourself. The Statement of Academic Purpose is required and describes your academic plans.  
> The statement is short (no more than two double-spaced pages, unless a maximum word or page count is specified by the department) and should be proofed carefully before you submit your application, as no updates or revisions will be accepted.

**Note:** Data Science did not specify a separate maximum word/page count beyond the GSAS default, so the binding limit is **two double-spaced pages**.

## Personal History Statement (optional)

> The Personal History Statement is to get to know you as an individual and as a potential graduate student. Please describe how your personal background has motivated you to pursue a graduate degree. Please note that the Personal History Statement is not meant to be a general autobiography.
>
> The statement is optional and should not exceed two double-spaced pages. It should not duplicate the Statement of Purpose.
