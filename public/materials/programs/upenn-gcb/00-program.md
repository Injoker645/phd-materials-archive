## Funding

Students accepted to BGS PhD programs receive a **fully funded fellowship** — tuition, fees, health insurance, and stipend — regardless of financial need. Support is generally for the **5–6 years** expected to finish.

**Annual stipend: $49,000, effective Fall 2026.**

Exception that does **not** apply to him: applicants who already hold an MD/PhD/other professional degree must self-fund. He does not.

## International applicants (FAQ, verbatim gist)

BGS recruits internationally but **can only accept a small number**. Competition is intense. They advise applying only if the program fits research interests and abilities. Successful applicants have extensive training in the proposed area, very strong records, and high TOEFL/IELTS if required.

WES credential evaluation is **optional**, not required.

## Materials (FAQ)

| Piece | Rule |
|---|---|
| Personal statement | Academic/career objectives; **~500 words**; be specific about area and why Penn |
| Research statement | Goals, approaches, results, implications; one or several projects; **~1000 words** |
| Community statement | Required (length not stated on FAQ) |
| CV | **Do not include GPA or GRE** |
| Letters | **3**; online Penn form. No Interfolio. Can submit app before letters arrive. 4th letter is added if it arrives |
| Transcripts | Unofficial uploads, English or translated. Current students: Spring 2026 grades + Fall 2026 enrollment. Official only after accept |
| GRE | **Not required and not accepted** for BGS (ignore GRE fields shared with other PSOM divisions) |
| English | TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo if native language is not English, **unless** degree from a US or other approved English-instructed institution (waiver is automatic in review — no request). No minimum score. Official scores by deadline. TOEFL older than 2 years must be retaken |
| TOEFL code | **2900** — “University of Pennsylvania - PSOM PhD, Master’s, and Certificates” |
| Faculty contact | **Not required** before applying. Rotations chosen with the graduate group after admission |
| After submit | Cannot replace statements/CV in the system; mailed updates may be added to the file only |
