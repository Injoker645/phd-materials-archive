# MIT — outreach

**Norm (SOURCED, EECS FAQ §17):** faculty will not discuss financial support or thesis supervision with students who have not yet been admitted. Post-admit matching involves interviews/visits.

**Implication:** do **not** write a "will you advise me?" email. If any contact happens, keep it to a one-paragraph pointer at shared interest (Ghassemi: measurement vs explanation) and the Dec 1 deadline. Expect silence. The application carries the weight.

Draft (optional, low priority vs NYU/Harvard/Columbia):

The project I want to work on is a per-prediction audit of clinical models that separates site-invariant evidence from site-specific signal, specifically because I take seriously the argument that current explainability is the wrong trust substitute in clinical settings. I will not ask about advising before admission. If a short conversation before the deadline would be useful on your end, I am happy to; otherwise I will put the argument in the essays.
