- Admissions are **committee-based**; advisors are not assigned at admit. **SOURCED** https://www.cs.cornell.edu/phd-computer-science/apply
- Tech affiliation follows the **thesis advisor's campus**. **SOURCED** Tech PhD pages
- SoP should name a small set (2–4) with distinct roles: health systems / digital health, clinical ML methods, responsible AI / evaluation. Do not invent a formal co-advising structure Cornell does not publish.
