# Cornell CS / Tech - outreach

## Norms

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Advisor assignment is **post-admission**; committee admits to the department | **SOURCED** https://www.cs.cornell.edu/phd-computer-science/apply |
| "It is not necessary to discuss your application with our faculty members before you apply, although it is not harmful to do so." | **SOURCED** same FAQ |
| Application form asks who you have been in contact with / faculty of interest; listing faculty of interest is helpful even without prior dialogue | **SOURCED** same |

**Implication:** Cold email is optional, not required. Keep any note short. Do not ask "will you advise me?" Do not ask about funding. Expect silence; the application carries the weight.

Priority vs other schools: lower than NYU / Harvard AIM diligence / Columbia funding confirmation. One or two Tech/Weill notes max if bandwidth remains.

## What not to email

- Funding / COLA / stipend dollar questions to faculty (use Grad School stipend page + offer letter).
- "Can I skip Ithaca?" before he has a Tech advisor willing to sponsor the petition.
- Comparisons to NYU Langone or prestige flattery.
- Attachments, thesis PDFs, or multi-page research proposals on first contact.
- Any version of the KFSH electrophoresis pilot.
