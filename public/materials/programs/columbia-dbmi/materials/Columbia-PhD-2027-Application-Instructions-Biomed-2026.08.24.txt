              Instructions for Completing the Application

    · Online Application
    · $110 Application Fee
    · Personal Statement (500-1,000 words recommended)
    · 3 Letters of Recommendation from academic sources (online)
    · Transcripts (unofficial copies uploaded with application)

For an expeditious review of your application, we require that all materials be submitted online. Unofficial copies
of transcripts must be uploaded as part of the online application and official copies will be required for accepted
students. All tests should be taken at least six weeks before the application deadline to ensure timely receipt by
the admissions office. Additional supporting materials should be submitted as they become available.
Supporting documents will not be accepted after the deadline date. All documents become the property of
Columbia University and cannot be returned. Please visit our website for application guidelines and Q&A.

Note that applicants must submit all materials directly, not through an agent or third-party vendor, with the
following exceptions: submissions by the US Department of State's Fulbright Program and its three partner
agencies IIE, LASPAU and AMIDEAST; submissions by the Danish-American Fulbright Commission (DAF); and
submissions by Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst (DAAD). In addition, applicants are required to attest
to the accuracy and authenticity of all information and documents submitted to Columbia. If you have any
questions about this requirement, please contact the Vagelos Institute PhD Pathway
(BiomedicalSciences@cumc.columbia.edu).

We require that the work contained in your application (including Personal Statement) is completely accurate
and exclusively your own. Columbia University permits the use of generative AI tools for idea generation and/or
to edit a candidate's work; however, using these tools to generate complete responses violates the Honor Code.

Failure to submit complete, accurate, and authentic application documents consistent with these instructions
may result in denial or revocation of admission, cancellation of academic credit, suspension, expulsion, or
eventual revocation of degree. Applicants may be required to assist admissions staff and faculty involved in
admission reviews in the verification of all documents and statements made in documents submitted by students
as part of the application review process.

Additionally, the applicant has an obligation to update their application, from the point of submission until
enrollment at the University, of any material changes to their application, including change in standing at any
academic institution (includes current school, even if it is another Columbia University program) due to academic
or behavior issues. If the application asks for information about criminal history, then any pending criminal
charges or subsequent convictions would also be material changes.

Fall Term/Full-time

All PhD programs at the Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons are full-time programs,
and entrance is only in the fall term. Applications should be submitted by December 1, 2026. The application
portal will be closed at 5:00 PM EST. The Biomedical Informatics program also offers a terminal MA degree.
You may use this application for that program as well.

Application Fee

The $110 nonrefundable application fee can be paid by credit card at the time of application. Once submitted,
your application fee is not refundable, with the exception when duplicate payment is made for the same
application for the same admissions cycle. Applications are not considered complete and will not be reviewed
until the fee is paid and the application is submitted. Our programs will waive the application fee for: (1)
applicants who demonstrate financial need or personal hardship; and (2) applicants who are currently serving in
the US military. Please email your request to BiomedicalSciences@cumc.columbia.edu before you submit
your application with materials to support your request, such as letters from your university, tax returns, or other
financial documents. Important: under no circumstances should you include your Social Security Number in any
of the documents submitted via this form. If your Social Security Number or other sensitive, personal information
appears on any of your financial materials, please redact before submitting.

Personal Statement

A Personal Statement is a 500-1,000-word (recommended) essay explaining your academical and career goal,
relevant research or work history, and why you want to attend Columbia and a specific program/track.

Transcripts

Please upload a copy of your unofficial transcripts with the online application. If you are currently in school,
please submit an updated transcript reflecting fall 2026 grades as soon as available. All documents not issued
in English by the official signing them must be accompanied by English translations. Official copies of transcripts
will be requested for accepted students only. Please visit our website for instructions for submission of
transcripts when accepting an offer of admission.

Letters of Recommendation

Three letters of recommendation from academic sources are required. Letters should be from your most recent
instructors or research mentors in your proposed field of study. If you have been out of school, one letter should
be from your employer, supervisor, or person familiar with your most recent activities. If you have attended
another graduate school, one letter must be from the graduate instructor/mentor most familiar with your work.
Letters should be submitted online. If this presents a problem, please contact our office. The email address for
each recommender should be a professional email address (e.g., with an ".edu" extension or company-related
domain). If such an email address is not possible, the student must provide an explanation and seek permission
from this office to utilize a non-professional email address for the reference. Recommenders should be
encouraged to include in their letters: their relationship to the applicant; the duration of their relationship; and as
many specifics regarding their experience as possible (for example, a former instructor might name the course
that the applicant took with them, the applicant's performance in the class, ranking of the applicant against other
students taught, etc.). Applicants should not participate in the drafting of letter(s) of recommendation. The IP
address from which the recommendation is submitted must be different from the IP address from which the
application is submitted. If this is not the case, the letter will be verified as submitted by the recommender and/or
the applicant must supply a new letter.

Graduate Record Examination (GRE)

General and Advanced (Subject) GRE scores are not required by any of our programs/tracks, although you may
submit them if you apply.

If you choose to submit General GRE and/or Advanced (Subject) GRE scores, they should be no more than five
years old. Our institution code is 2619. No department code is required.

English Proficiency Requirement

Applicants whose undergraduate degree was awarded by an institution in a country where English is not the
sole official language must complete the English proficiency requirement. The English proficiency
requirement is based on the official language of the country in which the undergraduate (or Bachelor's)
degree was earned, regardless of an applicant's citizenship. The English proficiency requirement can be
fulfilled through:

    · The Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) or the International English Language Testing
         System (IELTS). For TOEFL, our School Code is 2619. A minimum TOEFL score of 100 on the
         internet-based test or a 7.5 for the IELTS is required for all admitted applicants. TOEFL and IELTS
         scores are valid for two years after the test date. We accept the "MyBest" scores reporting option offered
         by TOEFL(iBT). You may use the results of tests taken on various dates to achieve the minimum score
         requirement. If an applicant's score is below the above minimum, the application won't be
         reviewed.

    · Students who have earned a Master's degree, but not an undergraduate or Bachelor's degree, in the US
         or another country whose sole official language is English are required to complete the English proficiency
         requirement; however, if you took the TOEFL or IELTS exam when applying to a graduate program at
         another institute and scored above the minimum requirement of 100 (TOEFL) or 7.5 (IELTS), you may
         fulfill the English proficiency requirement by uploading a scanned copy of your old score report to the
         online application.
    · If you earned your undergraduate degree in a country where the official language is not English, but the
         official language of instruction at your institution is English, you may request a waiver of the English
         proficiency requirement to BiomedicalSciences@cumc.columbia.edu with a letter from your university.
