# Columbia DBMI — outreach

## Norms

Confirm on DBMI apply page whether faculty contact is encouraged. Default for BMI programmes: brief, specific, one email max, no attachment spam.

## Pre-application diligence email (funding — send this)

To: BiomedicalSciences@cumc.columbia.edu (or Grad Program Manager listed on DBMI site)

> Hi — I am an international applicant (Pakistani national) preparing a Fall 2027 application to the Biomedical Informatics PhD. I understand NLM training seats are limited to US citizens and permanent residents. Could you confirm whether admitted international PhD students in Biomedical Informatics receive the same multi-year GSAS / departmental tuition-and-stipend package as domestic students when they are not NLM-eligible, and for how many years that guarantee typically runs? Thank you.

**Do not submit the application treating H5 as bulletproof until this is answered in writing.**
