## Funding — NLM vs parallel path (critical)

### NLM training seats (closed to you)

- DBMI runs a long-standing NLM-funded training programme. **SOURCED** [Student Funding](https://www.dbmi.columbia.edu/student-funding-2/); [Apply FAQ](https://www.dbmi.columbia.edu/apply/).
- **Per NIH, NLM eligibility is limited to US citizens and permanent residents.** **SOURCED** same pages. Pakistani / F-1 → **ineligible**.

### Parallel GSAS / departmental path for internationals (your path)

What official pages actually say:

1. **"The Columbia University Graduate School of Arts and Sciences (GSAS) guarantees funding for all Ph.D. students under certain conditions"** (time-to-degree limits). Funding covers **tuition, health insurance fees, University fees, and a stipend**. **SOURCED** [Student Funding](https://www.dbmi.columbia.edu/student-funding-2/); [DBMI apply](https://www.dbmi.columbia.edu/apply/).
2. Payment mechanism may be **NLM fellow** (stipend 3×/year) **or Graduate Research Assistant (GRA)** (biweekly salary). **"the yearly amount received is identical"** and is set by the Board of Trustees each summer; amount appears in the **admission offer letter**. Annual increases "usually in the vicinity of 3 percent" but **not guaranteed**. **SOURCED** [Student Funding](https://www.dbmi.columbia.edu/student-funding-2/).
3. Vagelos / VIBRE PhD Pathways page: **"All PhD students"** receive full support for tuition, university fees, student health services, and medical insurance; **"You will also receive a generous stipend (at least $47,137 for FY25-26)"**. **SOURCED** [How to Apply — Current Student Stipend and Benefits](https://www.vagelos.columbia.edu/education/academic-programs/vagelos-institutes-biomedical-research-education-phd-programs/becoming-student/how-apply). Treat **$47,137** as the published **FY25–26 floor**, not a promise for Fall 2027. Do not invent a higher number.

### Loud residual risks (do not soft-pedal)

- **Offer-letter dependence:** dollar amount and multi-year duration are communicated in the Dean's offer, not as a public schedule for internationals. **SOURCED** funding page; exact international multi-year length **UNCERTAIN** beyond "guarantee … under certain conditions."
- **GRA vs fellowship packaging:** internationals are the population that lives on GRA / institutional packages when NLM is unavailable. That path is **named**, but **cohort composition / historical international admit rate for DBMI is not verified** in this folder (**UNCERTAIN** — email BiomedicalSciences@cumc.columbia.edu or Grad Program Manager before treating H5 as bulletproof).

Email BiomedicalSciences@cumc.columbia.edu before submit. **He matches neither.** $110 stands. Older PDF hardship language is **not** what he will rely on. Program is backburner (fit was not high enough to pay).
