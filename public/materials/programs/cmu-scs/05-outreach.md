## Talking points if a call happens

1. Audit framing, not another DG trainer (clinical DG often fails to beat pooled ERM).
2. Prevalence-difference pre-registration as a required first step (CausTab-style boundary condition).
3. Decision wrap: conformal / deploy-or-recalibrate, answering "diagnostic without a decision."
4. Honest gap: no prior multi-site EHR study end to end.
5. Ask how ACMI / Wilder lab currently reach clinical environments (without claiming entitlement to UPMC data).
