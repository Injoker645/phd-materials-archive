## Funding (as provided)

- Fully funded for admitted PhD students: **~$41,000 stipend / year**, **100% tuition**, **health insurance**.
- Dept. attempts to fund all admitted candidates and match students to funded projects.
- **NLM / NIH training grants:** ~13 slots, **US only** — does **not** remove the base stipend package for internationals (NLM is a specific award type, not the only path).
- Treat dollar amount as published-cycle language until the offer letter; confirm for Fall 2027.
