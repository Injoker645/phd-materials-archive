# Mount Sinai AIET — outreach

## Norms

Biomedical Sciences PhD admissions are typically committee-managed with rotations after enrolment. Treat faculty email as optional diligence, not a soft admit. Prefer one short note, specific, no attachment spam. Confirm current contact addresses on live Icahn pages before sending.

Default: **do not** mass-email every AI and Human Health faculty member.

---

## Pre-application diligence email (funding: send this if still anxious)

Official page already says internationals in PhD Biomedical Sciences / Neuroscience are eligible for full institutional funding. Use this only if he wants multi-year duration restated before ranking offers.

To: grads@mssm.edu (or the live Graduate School admissions contact listed on the portal)

> Hi, I am an international applicant (Pakistani national) preparing a Fall 2027 application to the PhD in Biomedical Sciences, with interest in the AIET training area. Your international financial aid page states that PhD Biomedical Sciences students are eligible for full institutional funding. Could you confirm whether that package (tuition and stipend) is the standard offer for admitted international students, and for how many years it typically runs while students remain in good standing? Thank you.

## Optional faculty note (only if he wants a research conversation)

One email max to Nadkarni **or** Glicksberg, not both in the same week unless the first reply suggests the other.

Template:

> Hi Dr. [Name], I am applying Fall 2027 to Mount Sinai's PhD in Biomedical Sciences (AIET). I am working on a narrow measurement problem: for one clinical prediction, estimating how much evidence is site-invariant versus site-specific, with a prevalence-shift check and a deploy / recalibrate wrap. Your work on [InfEHR / EHR ML: pick one concrete paper] is the closest published framing I have found to treating that as an operational question rather than a story after the fact. I am not asking for a commitment before admission. If you have two minutes, I would value knowing whether this audit angle is something your group would consider relevant for a rotating student. Brief CV available on request. Thank you.

**Do not** attach a five-page proposal. **Do not** compare NYU and Sinai in the email. **Do not** claim KFSH pilot work.
