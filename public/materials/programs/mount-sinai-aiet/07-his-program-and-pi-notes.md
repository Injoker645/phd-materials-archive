## How he would evaluate the programme

Not “does BMS include medical AI,” but: can he spend 5–6 years becoming someone who (1) understands technical behaviour of clinical AI, (2) investigates how healthcare institutions shape that behaviour, and (3) develops methods that make deployment safer and more equitable.

Legitimate intersection: trustworthy ML × clinical informatics × causal inference × health equity × implementation science × sociology of healthcare systems — but these are not interchangeable. Need a place that supports the **combination**.

### What Sinai offers (his map)

| Piece | Role |
|---|---|
| AIET inside BMS PhD | Technical research + biomedical applications; rotations before mentor choice |
| AI Assurance Lab | Validation, safety, accountability; post-deployment monitoring |
| Windreich AI & Human Health | Clinical implementation, ethical AI, disparities; EHR / ops / financial warehouse |

Infrastructure for more than accuracy-in-isolation exists. Whether a student can study **institutional incentives / operational priorities** still depends on supervisor, project scope, data access, and willingness.

### Three research goals (his table)

| Direction | Fit with published Sinai setup |
|---|---|
| Trustworthy clinical AI (provenance, spurious corr., causal, robustness) | Directly compatible |
| Real-world clinical impact (use, outcomes, post-deploy performance) | Compatible |
| Institutional incentives & inequality (reimbursement, occupancy, metrics shaping AI) | Possible via interdisciplinary collab; **not** the AI concentration’s centre |

Ambition = connect technical + institutional; risk = lab that only wants the first half.

### Three-layer project shape he wants

1. **What does the model learn?** Clinician-specific docs, local practices, demographic correlations, non-generalisable signals.  
2. **What happens in practice?** Decisions, trust, outcomes, performance across depts/hospitals.  
3. **What is the system incentivized to do?** Whether the optimised outcome tracks patient welfare vs reimbursement / ops / historical practice.

Example: readmission model — clinically useful factors vs cross-hospital change vs admission/discharge practices distorting prediction–outcome link.

### Pre-apply questions he cares about

1. Faculty who would supervise clinical AI robustness / provenance / causal reasoning?  
2. Realistic collab with clinicians / health-systems researchers on real-world outcomes & institutional practices?  
3. Would primary supervisor support a dissertation that questions whether the optimised outcome is good for patients?
