# Mayo Clinic — PhD Biomedical Sciences (hospital AI path)

**Compiled:** 2026-11-04. Considering candidate (mid-tier / realistic).

## Why it is on the considering pile

- **$0 application fee.** Fully funded for all appointed students: tuition waived + **$42,642** stipend (2026–27) + benefits.
- Hospital-embedded PhD (like Cedars) but **established** program with alumni — lower “new program” risk.
- AI path: Department of Artificial Intelligence and Informatics takes students via Biomedical Research Training; optional **AI in Healthcare (AIHC)** concentration includes *Ethical Issues in Artificial Intelligence and Information Technologies*.
- Closest listed track: **Clinical and Translational Science (CTS)**; after admission you can rotate into any eligible Mayo mentor regardless of track.
- Deadline **2 Dec 2026** (window opens 1 Sep). Campus declared at apply (Rochester MN / Phoenix AZ / Jacksonville FL); campus can change after rotations if mentor is elsewhere.
