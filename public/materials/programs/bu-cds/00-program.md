## What CDS is for (programme self-description)

- Prepare researchers for computational and data-driven processes across society, economy, and public discourse; extraction of insights from data and design of systems that make those insights actionable. **SOURCED** PhD degree page.
- Coursework spans foundational, applied, and **sociotechnical** dimensions of CDS. **SOURCED**
- Required **research rotations** with CDS-affiliated investigators; cohort ethics / RCR training; oral area exam; prospectus; thesis defence. **SOURCED**
- First two years: CDS departmental financial support while coursework, RCR, First Year Doctoral Seminar, and **two lab rotations** happen. Advisor(s) fund from year 3. **SOURCED** FAQs.
- Holistic review; applicants from core CDS fields (CS, ECE, Math & Stats) and applied domains (including bioinformatics and related). **SOURCED** PhD degree page.
