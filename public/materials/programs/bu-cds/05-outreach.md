# Outreach — BU CDS

> CDS admits by **central committee**. Students list potential advisors but enter **unbound**; advisor by end of year 2. **SOURCED** https://www.bu.edu/cds-faculty/programs-admissions/phd-degree/faqs/
> Faculty cold-email is **not banned** the way Harvard AIM bans it, but it is also **not required**. At least one CDS faculty page (Goldner) tells applicants not to email for permission to list them, and to apply through the CDS portal. Treat soft contact as optional; the application carries the weight.

---

## Norm summary

| Action | Guidance | Status |
|---|---|---|
| List 2–3 potential advisors on the application | Encouraged | **SOURCED** FAQs |
| Expect to enter without a locked advisor | Programme design | **SOURCED** FAQs |
| Mass "will you advise / fund me?" emails | Low value; committee decides | Practice guidance |
| Short interest note to one faculty whose papers he has actually read | Optional; expect silence | Soft |
| Programme questions | cds-admissions@bu.edu | **SOURCED** PhD page |
| Fee waiver request | cds-admissions@bu.edu with justification | **SOURCED** FAQs |

Do **not** email faculty on the FAQ "not currently accepting" list as if they are open seats.

Moaaz Tameer Islam

## Allowed email — programme office

Use for residual questions the site does not settle.

**To:** cds-admissions@bu.edu  
**Subject:** CDS PhD Fall 2027 — international funding / materials question

I have read the CDS PhD, funding, and FAQ pages and still have one concrete question: [one question].

Brief context: I am an international applicant (Pakistani; MSc Data Science, Uppsala) applying for Fall 2027. My research interest is per-prediction provenance for clinical models, as an audit layer rather than a new training method.

Thank you for any pointer to the right place on the site if I missed it.

Moaaz Tameer Islam

### Questions worth asking (pick one per email)

1. Can you confirm that admitted international students receive the standard Charles River / CDS 5-year full funding package (tuition, fees, SHIP premium, stipend)?
2. Is there a recommended length or prompt text for the Research Statement beyond what appears on the admissions-requirements page?
3. For applicants with degrees taught in English outside the US/UK list, what language-of-instruction documentation do you prefer alongside transcripts?

Do not ask them to evaluate his thesis or to introduce him to faculty.
