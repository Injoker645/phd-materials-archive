## Spectrum answer (read this first)

AIM is **quantitatively rigorous by design** — the FAQ says if you lack CS/AI-ML research experience, it is not the right programme. That does **not** mean every faculty member is "build another foundation model."

Rough spectrum on the official AIM faculty list:

| Pole | Who | What "practical" means here |
|---|---|---|
| **Most clinical / real-world-data / causal** | **Sebastian Schneeweiss** (co-director) | Pharmacoepi + comparative effectiveness on longitudinal claims/EHR-style data; causal inference for treatment decisions in real care. Closest to CARE-AI / readmissions / "does this change a clinical decision" framing. |
| **Values, fairness, deployment validity** | **Arjun Manrai** | Clinical decision-making across populations; reproducibility and adoption challenges of medical AI; dataset transparency / bias consensus work. Closest to his trustworthy-AI / bias / "who signs off" angle. |
| **Reliable clinical AI / evaluation** | **Pranav Rajpurkar** | Evaluation frameworks, clinician–AI collaboration, certification pathways — deployment-side twin of provenance/audit ideas. Still computational, but the *questions* are practical. |
| **Multi-site EHR methods** | **Tianxi Cai** | Statistical/ML mining of multi-institutional EHR; phenotyping and cross-system inference. The empirical home for site/provenance-style measurement. |
| **Programme / systems vision** | **Isaac Kohane** (co-director) | Healthcare-as-living-laboratory; AI-in-medicine agenda; NEJM AI. Name for programme fit, not as "I will only join Kohane Lab." |
| **More modality / methods-forward** | Zitnik, Lotter, Mahmood, Yu, Patel | Strong labs, but farther from his current provenance/trustworthy-EHR pitch (networks/FMs, medical imaging CV, digital pathology, cancer multi-omics, exposome). Fine as secondary interest; do not force into SoP. |

**Bottom line for him:** He is not stuck with "only computation for its own sake." The practical/clinical side of AIM is real (Schneeweiss + Manrai + Rajpurkar + Cai), but the *entry ticket* is still quantitative ML/stats strength — which his MSc DS + CARE-AI role cover. He should pick Faculty of Interest from the left/middle of this spectrum, not invent a soft clinical-informatics identity the FAQ rejects.
