# Outreach - Harvard AIM

> **Hard rule:** Do **not** cold-email AIM faculty.
>
> **SOURCED** (DBMI AIM track page): "Prospective applicants: Please do not contact AIM faculty. Please read all the information found on our main program website and if you still have questions email AIM@hms.harvard.edu. Thank you."
> https://dbmi.hms.harvard.edu/education/phd-program/ai-medicine-phd-track
>
> Same instruction on the GSAS Biomedical Informatics contact block (AIM@hms.harvard.edu). **SOURCED** https://gsas.harvard.edu/program/biomedical-informatics

This is the opposite of the NYU / many CS-department cultures. Violating it is an easy self-reject signal.

---

## What to do instead

| Action | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Read the main BMI/AIM site and FAQs fully before asking anything | Explicitly requested | **SOURCED** |
| Email **AIM@hms.harvard.edu** only for residual programme questions the site does not answer | Sanctioned channel | **SOURCED** |
| Attend AIM / BMI virtual information sessions and recruitment events | Applying-aim page points applicants to recruitment events. **He signed up for a webinar/seminar (2026-09-22)** — log takeaways in `verification-notes.md`. | **SOURCED** https://bmiphd.hms.harvard.edu/applying-aim |
| Name faculty and fit **inside the SoP** (and later interviews), not in unsolicited email | How AIM wants interest expressed | Practice guidance from no-contact policy |
| If invited to interview, then ask faculty-specific questions | Contact becomes mutual | Standard; timing **UNCERTAIN** until invite |

---

## Do **not** send

- "Dear Professor Kohane / Rajpurkar / Cai / Manrai, I am interested in your lab..."
- Thesis PDFs, GitHub dumps, or "quick chat" requests to AIM faculty before an official invite
- Parallel cold emails to BIG faculty hoping for AIM adjacency (**UNCERTAIN** whether BIG has a different contact norm; do not test it as a workaround for AIM)

---

## Allowed email template - programme office only

Use only if a concrete question remains after reading the site (examples: whether Personal Statement is required this cycle; whether a writing sample is expected; confirmation that international admits receive the standard DMS package). Keep it short. Register A. No em dash.

**To:** AIM@hms.harvard.edu
**Subject:** AIM PhD Fall 2027 - application materials question

I have read the AIM and GSAS Biomedical Informatics pages and still have one concrete question the FAQ does not settle: [one question].

Brief context only if needed: I am an international applicant (Pakistani; MSc Data Science, Uppsala) applying for Fall 2027 to the AIM track. My research interest is per-prediction provenance for clinical models, measuring site-invariant versus site-specific evidence as an audit layer rather than as a new training method.

Thank you for any pointer to the right place on the site if I missed it.

Moaaz Tameer Islam

---

### Questions worth asking the office (pick one per email)

1. For the Fall 2027 cycle, is the GSAS Personal Statement required for Biomedical Informatics / AIM?
2. Is a writing sample required or optional for AIM?
3. Can you confirm that admitted international students receive the standard HMS/DMS full tuition-and-stipend package (i.e. funding is not limited to NLM-eligible trainees)?

Do not ask them to evaluate his thesis or to introduce him to faculty.

## Info-session talking points (verbal)

If a session includes Q&A:

1. How independent are multi-hospital environments inside one Boston system for studying site-specific documentation effects?
2. How early do AIM students typically get access to multi-institutional EHR resources under co-mentorship?
3. How does the programme want applicants to express faculty fit given the no-cold-email rule? (SoP naming only? Interview only?)
