# Cedars-Sinai — PhD in Health Artificial Intelligence

**Compiled:** 2026-11-04. Added as the mid-tier / realistic healthcare-AI slot after MIT and Berkeley were archived.

## Why it is on the list

- Direct **Health AI** PhD embedded in an academic medical center (first US hospital-embedded Health AI PhD of this kind, accredited 2025).
- Curriculum includes **Ethical AI**, bias/fairness, explainability, clinical immersion — matches responsible/safe clinical AI framing.
- **Fully funded** for all admitted students: tuition + ~$52k stipend (12-month) for four years + health insurance. Students are on employee-style packages but focus on training/dissertation.
- **International students approved** to enroll.
- Application fee **$55**; waiver by emailing `GroupGradProgram@cshs.org` (or `gradprogram@csmc.edu` per resources page) with a short hardship paragraph.
- Deadline **31 Jan 2027** — later than Dec 1 schools, which fits draft-early / upload-late + CARE-AI material.

## Research / fit notes (high level)

- Co-mentoring: primary **AI mentor** + **clinical co-mentor**.
- Year 1: AI/ML, Ethical AI, Computational Biomedicine, research + clinical rotations.
- Faculty/themes worth a later deep-dive: CAIRE (AutoML, fair/unbiased methods); Tiffani Bright (bias detection/correction in clinical ML); translational clinical AI deployment.
- Different from a pure CS PhD or a classical BMI informatics PhD: hospital-embedded, clinical immersion required.
