Applicants to the CIS Doctoral Program are considered for available funding; it is not necessary to submit extra material to be considered for funding." Research support is framed as **research fellow** positions (not classic RA line items); teaching via Teaching Practicum. **SOURCED** [CIS how to apply](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/graduate/how-to-apply/); [Fellowships + Aid](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/phd/fellowships-aid/).

No US-citizen-only restriction on that doctoral paragraph.

4. **Application fee:** $95; automatic waivers listed for US citizens/PRs with proven low income, Penn/US military affiliation, or eligible Accelerated Master's partner applicants. **SOURCED** [How to Apply](https://gradadm.seas.upenn.edu/how-to-apply/). ## Statement / SOP prompts and length

SEAS asks one personal statement (≤2 pages) answering:

2. What have you done that makes you a strong candidate?  
3. How will you benefit?  
4. How will you contribute to the student community?  
5. Why will you succeed?  
6. What will you do after the programme?
