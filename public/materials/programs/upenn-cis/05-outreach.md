# UPenn CIS — outreach

## Norms

SEAS / CIS pages reviewed for this folder do **not** publish a Harvard-AIM-style ban on faculty contact. Default for CIS PhD applications: brief, specific, one email max per person, no attachment spam, no "will you advise me / fund me."

Admissions are still department-managed; a reply is a courtesy, not a soft admit. Put the intellectual match in the personal statement either way.

## Pre-application diligence email (funding — optional but useful)

To: CIS Doctoral Program Manager (Britton Carnevali — confirm live email on [CIS doctoral](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/doctoral/)) or SEAS Grad Admissions

> Hi, I am an international applicant (Pakistani national) preparing a Fall 2027 application to the CIS PhD. Penn Engineering's funding page states that doctoral students receive full funding for the duration of the programme while in good academic standing. Could you confirm that this package (tuition, fees, health and dental insurance, and living wage) applies the same way to international CIS PhD admits, and for how many years that support typically runs? Thank you.

**Do not invent a stipend number in any email or SOP.**

## Optional faculty note (Johnson — highest priority if any)

One paragraph only. Send once, early enough that silence is fine.

> Hi Professor Johnson, I am applying to the CIS PhD for Fall 2027. The project I want to work on is a per-prediction audit for clinical models that separates site-invariant evidence from site-specific documentation and operational signal, tied to a deploy / recalibrate / escalate decision rather than to a post-hoc story. Your AI-4-AI lab is the closest place I have found where that measurement would have to survive contact with real ambulatory documentation and deployment constraints. I am not asking about advising before admission. If a short conversation before the December deadline would be useful on your end, I am happy to; otherwise I will put the argument in the statement.
