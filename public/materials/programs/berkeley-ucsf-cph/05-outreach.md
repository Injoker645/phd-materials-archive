# Outreach — CPH faculty contact

---

## Does CPH encourage faculty contact?

**Yes.** Official admissions guidance:

1. Schedule an information session with the Graduate Student Advisor.  
2. Research core faculty for alignment.  
3. It is **possible, although not mandatory**, to reach out to potential faculty sponsors about openness to graduate students or lab rotations if admitted.

FAQ explicitly: applicants **may reach out to more than one professor**; the application asks for **top 3 faculty**; contact is introductory, not a formal interview; faculty are not expected to commit to advising at this stage.

**SOURCED:** https://computationalhealth.berkeley.edu/admissions/

Also: PhD program page encourages reaching out to AGG faculty and `cph.info@berkeley.edu`.  
**SOURCED:** https://computationalhealth.berkeley.edu/phd-program/

**Contrast:** This is *not* like programs that forbid cold email. Draft outreach.

## What not to do

- Mass-mail the entire AGG with identical text.
- Attach a 10-page proposal.
- Claim UC data access you don’t have.
- Mention the fabricated electrophoresis pilot.
- Flatter “world-famous” labs.
- Write as if they’ve already agreed to supervise.

---

## If they reply

Ask: Are you taking rotation students? Any active multi-site / UCSF Health deployment evaluations where provenance or shift measurement would help? Anything I should read before listing you as top-3?

If no reply: still list them if the fit is real; CPH does not require prior contact.
