## FAQ distill (2026-10-21)

- Faculty contact: email **more than one** professor. The application asks for a **top 3**. Contact is an intro, not an interview, and nobody is committed.
- Clinical vs biology: geared to **clinical impact**, and also interested in clinical data that yields mechanistic insight. No bright line between discovery and clinical.
- Classes: mostly **in person**. Required classes are scheduled with time to cross the Bay. Occasional Zoom.
- No master’s en route, and no master’s required to apply.
- Careers: advisor networks, Frontiers conference, Berkeley and UCSF career centers. Paths include faculty, industry research, clinical AI in health systems, public-sector health tech.
- One application per Berkeley campus. CPH uses the Berkeley portal, so he cannot also apply to another Berkeley program this cycle. A separate UCSF program is allowed.
- Portal: https://gradapp.berkeley.edu/apply/

### Transit (car-free, Bay Area)

Car ownership is **not required**. Typical Berkeley ↔ UCSF Mission Bay pattern:

1. **BART** Downtown Berkeley → **16th St Mission** (or Civic Center / Powell depending on shuttle).
2. **UCSF free shuttle** (ID-based): **Red** line 16th St BART ↔ Mission Bay (~20 min per UCSF transport materials).
3. **Parnassus / Mt Zion / ZSFG:** UCSF **Blue / Gold / Grey / Lime** shuttles interconnect campuses; Muni (e.g. N Judah to Parnassus) is an alternative.

Sources:

- **SOURCED (FAQ hybrid):** https://computationalhealth.berkeley.edu/admissions/
- **SOURCED (UCSF shuttle overview):** https://liveshuttle.ucsf.edu/
- **SOURCED (Red shuttle / 16th St):** https://campuslifeserviceshome.ucsf.edu/sites/cls3/files/2022-09/TRN-Red.pdf

**UNCERTAIN:** Exact door-to-door commute time Berkeley ↔ Parnassus on a given weekday (depends on BART + shuttle + walking). Budget ~60–90+ minutes one way as a planning assumption, not a sourced figure.
